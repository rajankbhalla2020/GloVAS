Frequently Asked Questions

Help & Subscription Management

Unsubscribe Guide *305#

How do I unsubscribe from any Glo service?

To unsubscribe, dial *305# from your Glo line and follow the prompts.

How do I unsubscribe from a service? +
To unsubscribe from any active service, simply dial *305# on your Glo phone and follow the on-screen prompts. You will see a list of your active subscriptions with the option to opt out instantly.
What are Glo Value Added Services (VAS)? +
Glo Value Added Services provide mobile entertainment, knowledge, and lifestyle features including mobile gaming tournaments, academic e-learning courses, Afrobeats streaming, live football updates, lottery jackpot draws, caller tunes, and Borrow Me Credit.
How do I subscribe to a service? +
Select your preferred pack (Daily, Weekly, or Monthly) on any service page, choose Auto-Renewal or One-off, and tap SUBSCRIBE NOW to dial the code directly from your phone.
What is the difference between Auto-Renewal and One-off? +
Auto-Renewal: Your pack automatically renews upon expiration provided you have sufficient airtime balance.
One-off: The subscription expires naturally without any automatic deduction.
How do I borrow emergency airtime or data? +
Visit the Borrow Me Credit section. Dial *303*1# to borrow emergency airtime or *303*2# to borrow data. The borrowed credit is deducted upon your next recharge.
How can I reach Glo Customer Care? +
You can dial *305# free of charge from any Glo SIM to manage all your services, or contact Glo Customer Support.
